Scan and read in English

Hold your phone over the panel. NutriFacts finds it, captures it on its own, and translates every line.

See how much is a lot

Japanese labels have no daily values. We add them, with high and low levels, from the guidelines you choose: Japan, EU, US or Canada.

Honest about what’s missing

Most labels only list five lines. When sugar or fibre isn’t printed, we say “not disclosed”, never zero.

A grade you can trust

A simple A–E score from official thresholds. If the label leaves things out, you see a range, not a guess.

Salt, and sodium too

Japan prints salt equivalent (食塩相当量). We convert it to sodium in mg, so you can compare it with home.

Private by design

Your scan history is saved only on your iPhone. Choose how labels are read, including Apple Intelligence.

Why it matters

糖質 isn’t sugar.

It’s the most common mistranslation on Japanese packs. 糖質 means net carbs, starch included. 糖類 means sugars. And most labels don’t print either.

NutriFacts shows you exactly how much of the carbohydrate figure the label explains, and how much it leaves out.

Questions

What is TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple’s free app for trying beta versions of iPhone apps. When the beta opens, we’ll email you a link. Tap it on your iPhone to install TestFlight and NutriFacts.

Which iPhones are supported?

NutriFacts needs iOS 26 or later. Reading labels with Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone that supports it.

Does it cost anything?

The beta is free.

Do I need to read Japanese?

No. That’s the point. NutriFacts is made for people living in or visiting Japan who want to know what’s in their food.

What will you do with my email?

We’ll use it to send you the beta invite and occasional updates about NutriFacts. We won’t share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
